Argentine Desserts: Grilled Pancakes & Dulce de Leche Alfajores

No authentic Argentine asado is truly complete without a sweet finale. At Criollo Grill, we bring the most iconic Argentine desserts directly to your event in Copenhagen, combining the warmth of live fire with the unmistakable flavor of dulce de leche.

Grilled Pancakes with Dulce de Leche or Nutella

Prepared warm right on the iron griddle over the embers, our artisan pancakes are generously filled with authentic Argentine dulce de leche or melted Nutella. Rolled and warmed to perfection over the grill, they are a live-cooking spectacle and an irresistible treat for guests of all ages.

Artisan Dulce de Leche Alfajores

Alongside our warm pancakes, we offer the undisputed king of Argentine patisserie: handmade alfajores de dulce de leche. Soft, melt-in-your-mouth cookies sandwiching a thick layer of creamy dulce de leche—ideal to enjoy with coffee or at the end of the celebration.
